# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 21, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how I collect, use, store, and protect information when you use my Discord bot.

By using my bot, you acknowledge the data practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information I Collect

- **Discord User ID:** Used to identify your account and associate your settings with you.
- **Epic Games Account Information:** Your Epic Games account ID and display name, used to provide account-related features.
- **Epic Games Authentication Tokens:** Used to access authorized Epic Games functionality without requiring your password.
- **Bot Preferences:** Settings and preferences associated with features you enable.

I do not ask for or store your Epic Games password.

## 3. How I Use Your Information

I collect information solely to provide and maintain my bot's functionality, including:

- Connecting your Discord account to your Epic Games account.
- Providing account-related commands and features.
- Saving your preferences.
- Maintaining the bot's functionality and security.

I do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

## 4. Data Storage and Security

I take reasonable measures to protect stored information and prevent unauthorized access.

Authentication tokens are sensitive credentials and are used only for the functionality you authorize.

## 5. Data Sharing

I do not sell your personal information.

Information may be transmitted to Discord and Epic Games as necessary to operate my bot's features.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

I retain account information only as long as necessary to provide my bot's services.

You can unlink your Epic Games account using:

`/logout`

Unlinking your account permanently deletes all stored information associated with your account and prevents the bot from accessing your Epic Games account.
